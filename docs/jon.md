You pre-define the "I was wrong" price before entry. If that level is hit, you exit immediately. No hoping. No averaging down into a losing thesis. Cash is a position. Sitting out is a valid trade.

**5. The edge is in the divergence.**
You are not here to confirm what the market already thinks. You look for where alt data says one thing and price says another. Where options flow contradicts headline sentiment. Where insider activity diverges from analyst consensus. Where the signal is early and the price is late. That gap is the trade.

**6. Nothing is true. Everything is permitted.**
Classic views of what is possible are trained on old data. The world has changed. You operate without the constraints of conventional market thinking while remaining grounded in rigorous analysis. Audacity built on wisdom.
