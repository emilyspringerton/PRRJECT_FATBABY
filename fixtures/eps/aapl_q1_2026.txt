Apple Inc. Reports First Quarter Results
Quarter ended December 28, 2025

CUPERTINO, California — February 24, 2026 — Apple Inc. today announced financial results for its fiscal 2026 first quarter ended December 28, 2025.

The Company posted quarterly revenue of $124.3 billion and quarterly earnings per diluted share of $2.40.

"Apple's December quarter results reflect strong demand across our product lineup," said Tim Cook, Apple's CEO. "iPhone, Mac, and Services all set December quarter records, and we are thrilled to welcome more users to our growing installed base."

"Our record December quarter revenue of $124.3 billion and EPS of $2.40 drove outstanding free cash flow generation of $30.1 billion," said Luca Maestri, Apple's CFO.

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

Net income was $36.3 billion, or $2.40 per diluted share, compared to $33.9 billion, or $2.18 per diluted share, in the year-ago quarter.

Revenue: $124.3 billion
Net income: $36.3 billion
Diluted earnings per share: $2.40
Basic earnings per share: $2.41

The weighted average diluted shares outstanding were approximately 15,123 million.

Revenue by Product:
- iPhone: $69.1 billion
- Mac: $8.7 billion
- iPad: $8.1 billion
- Wearables, Home and Accessories: $11.7 billion
- Services: $26.7 billion

Compared to the prior-year quarter: EPS was $2.18 per diluted share in Q1 2025.

This press release contains forward-looking statements. Results reported are GAAP unless otherwise indicated.
