Acme Financial Corp. Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results
Quarter ended June 30, 2026

NEW YORK — July 25, 2026 — Acme Financial Corp. today reported a net loss for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Net loss was $847 million, or $(0.42) per diluted share, compared to net income of $312 million, or $0.15 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter.

Revenue was $2.1 billion, down 18% from the prior year.

GAAP diluted earnings per share was $(0.42) for the quarter.

"While the second quarter reflected elevated credit loss provisions, our core franchise remains sound," said Jane Smith, CEO of Acme Financial Corp.

Weighted average diluted shares: 2,016 million.

Q2 2025 comparison: GAAP diluted EPS was $0.15 per diluted share.
