Microsoft Corp. Reports Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Results
Quarter ended December 31, 2025

REDMOND, Wash. — January 28, 2026 — Microsoft Corp. today announced the following results for the quarter ended December 31, 2025, as compared to the corresponding period of last fiscal year.

Revenue was $69.6 billion, an increase of 12% year-over-year.
Net income was $24.1 billion or $3.23 per diluted share.
GAAP diluted earnings per share was $3.23, compared to $2.93 per diluted share in the second quarter of fiscal year 2025.

Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share was $3.45 for the quarter.

"Our strong second quarter results were driven by continued AI-led demand across our cloud platform," said Satya Nadella, chairman and CEO of Microsoft.

"We delivered $69.6 billion in revenue and $3.23 earnings per diluted share," said Amy Hood, executive vice president and CFO of Microsoft.
