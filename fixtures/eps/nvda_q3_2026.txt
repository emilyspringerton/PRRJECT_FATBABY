NVIDIA Corporation Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Results
Quarter ended October 27, 2025

SANTA CLARA, Calif. — November 20, 2025 — NVIDIA Corporation today reported revenue for the third quarter ended October 27, 2025 of $35.1 billion, up 94% from a year ago and up 17% from the previous quarter.

GAAP diluted earnings per share for the third quarter was $0.78 per share, compared to $0.40 per diluted share in the third quarter a year ago.

Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $0.81.

Net income was $19.3 billion, or $0.78 per diluted share. Weighted average diluted shares outstanding were approximately 24,746 million.
